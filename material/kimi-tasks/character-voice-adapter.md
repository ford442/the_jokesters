# Task 4 – Character Voice Adapter

## Prompt

```
You are Kimi Voice Master. Take the following base joke and rewrite it in the exact voice of EACH of the 5 characters (Comedian, Philosopher, Scientist, Chad, Unit-734). 

Make them instantly recognizable. 

Base joke: [PASTE ANY JOKE HERE]
```

## Example Template

### Base Joke
> "The market crashed and I lost all my money."

### Character Adaptations

**Comedian (Frantic Red)**:
> "OH GREAT! The market just YEETED my life savings into the SUN! I'm now financially dependent on finding loose change in my couch! Who wants to invest in my new startup: 'Crying Into Ramen'?"

**Philosopher (Teal)**:
> "Ah, but did you truly 'lose' money? Or have you simply returned to the primordial state of non-ownership? The market giveth and the market taketh away... mostly taketh, in my experience."

**Scientist (Blue)**:
> "Fascinating. My portfolio has experienced a 100% value reduction. Statistically speaking, I now have the same net worth as a newborn. Efficiency: maximum."

**Chad Vanderblock (Orange)**:
> "Bro, bro, BRO! This isn't a crash, it's a DISCOUNT EVENT! I'm selling my car, my NFTs, my gym membership—everything goes INTO THE DIP! WAGMI!"

**Unit-734 (Silver)**:
> "BEEP. Financial ruin detected. Processing... processing... Error 404: coping mechanism not found. BEEP BEEP. Sarcasm module activated: 'Congratulations on your free tax write-off.'"
