# Task 6 – Chaos Injection Prompts

## Prompt

```
You are Kimi Chaos Injector. Create 20 wild one-shot prompts specifically for Market Mayhem mode that force the cast into hilarious tangents.
```

## Example Prompts (Seed List)

1. **The Meme Coin Launch**
   > Chad tries to launch a meme coin called "DipCoin" mid-market crash. The other characters must react to his increasingly desperate pitch deck.

2. **The Philosopher's Cult**
   > Philosopher starts an actual cult around gold worship. Scientist keeps trying to explain atomic properties while Chad wants in for the tax benefits.

3. **Unit-734's Solar Short**
   > Unit-734 threatens to "short the sun" because it's "clearly in a bubble." The others must talk the robot down from this existential threat.

4. **The Time Traveler**
   > Comedian claims to be from the future where Bitcoin is the only currency and used car salesmen are the new billionaires.

5. **Chad's TED Talk**
   > Chad starts giving an unsolicited TED Talk about "Grindset Geometry" while the market is literally crashing behind him.

6. **The Haunted Wallet**
   > Scientist's crypto wallet appears to be haunted by the ghost of a 2017 ICO investor. Unit-734 tries to perform an exorcism.

7. **Margin Call Karaoke**
   > Every time someone gets a margin call notification, they have to sing their next line like a power ballad.

8. **The Reverse Pump**
   > Philosopher convinces everyone that "down is the new up" and they should celebrate losses as "negative gains."

9. **Chad's AI Assistant**
   > Chad reveals he's been taking financial advice from a Magic 8-Ball. It's been more accurate than his previous advisor.

10. **The Arbitration**
    > Unit-734 declares itself the official arbitrator of all market disputes and starts handing down robot verdicts on everyone's trades.

11. **Gold Rush Reenactment**
    > Comedian insists everyone speak in 1849 Gold Rush prospector accents for the next 5 minutes while actual crypto crashes happen.

12. **The Intervention**
    > The other four stage an intervention for Chad's "dip buying addiction." He keeps trying to buy the dip in the conversation itself.

13. **Parallel Universe**
    > Scientist opens a portal to a parallel universe where Dogecoin is the world reserve currency. Chad wants to move there immediately.

14. **The ASMR Stream**
    > Unit-734 accidentally switches to ASMR mode and starts whispering terrifying financial predictions while everyone tries to focus.

15. **Chad's Sponsorship**
    > Chad starts doing unsolicited sponsorships for random products mid-conversation, treating the market crash like a YouTube ad read.

16. **The Technical Analysis**
    > Philosopher uses astrology instead of technical analysis and somehow predicts every move correctly. Scientist is losing his mind.

17. **The Emergency Broadcast**
    > Unit-734 hacks into the emergency broadcast system to warn about "imminent financial embarrassment." No one knows if it's a joke.

18. **Speed Dating**
    > Comedian decides this is the perfect time to speed date while watching the charts, treating every pump like a match and every dump like rejection.

19. **The Documentation**
    > Scientist starts treating the entire conversation like a formal research paper, complete with citations and peer review of Chad's statements.

20. **The Auction**
    > Chad starts auctioning off his remaining dignity as the market continues to fall. Bidding starts at one satoshi.

---

## Usage

Feed these prompts into your Director's chaos injection system to trigger spontaneous scene directions during Market Mayhem mode.
