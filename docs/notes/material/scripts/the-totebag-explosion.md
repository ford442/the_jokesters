EXT. SUNLIT SIDEWALK - DAY

[Glaring midday light bleaches the concrete. Café tables spill out behind them.
 The city hums with the casual indifference of a Tuesday.]

Justine and Madelin walk into frame. Both are dressed as though they attended
a very important funeral at 3 a.m. and have not been home since.

Madelin carries a large canvas tote. It bulges in a way that is geometrically
incorrect. Something inside it is clearly skull‑shaped. It clunks with each step.

Jackalyn rounds the corner. She wears a pristine ivory coat. She holds a matte-
white polymer thermos. A brushed‑aluminum smartwatch catches the sun. She has a
small white poodle on a silver leash. She looks like she just stepped out of a
notification for a wellness app.

JACKALYN
(beaming)
Oh my GOD. You two! I was literally just thinking about you!

JUSTINE
(flatly)
That's wild. Crazy world.

JACKALYN
(glancing at smartwatch)
I'm meeting Sophie at that new brunch place on Clement at eleven‑thirty.
You should come. They do a lavender cortado.

MADELIN
(hoisting the tote higher)
Mmm.

JUSTINE
We might be busy.

JACKALYN
Busy with what? It's a Tuesday.

[Beat. The tote bag emits a low, resonant clunk as Madelin shifts her weight.]

JACKALYN
(tilting her head)
What's in the bag?

MADELIN
Art supplies.

JUSTINE
(simultaneously)
A melon.

[Silence.]

JACKALYN
...A melon.

MADELIN
A very large melon. For art.

JACKALYN
(skeptical but polite)
Okay. That's... a commitment.

[**PUNCH‑UP: The tote becomes a physical menace.**]

*The poodle, BISCUIT, circles the tote, sniffs, then **plops** down directly
in front of it, tail wagging like a metronome. The bag, now wobbling, leans
against a nearby lamppost. The lamppost **groans** under the unexpected weight.*

BISCUIT
(barks once, then freezes)

JACKALYN
Biscuit, come on.

[Biscuit does not come on. He lifts a paw, as if to push the tote away, but
the bag tilts forward, **knocking over a half‑full iced latte** on a café table
just off‑screen. The latte spills, creating a slow‑motion cascade of coffee
that lands **perfectly** on the tote’s side, soaking the canvas.*

MADELIN
(quickly, desperate)
It’s… it’s a *portable hydro‑art installation*!

JUSTINE
(playing along, deadpan)
Yeah. The coffee is *the medium*.

JACKALYN
(raising an eyebrow)
Portable… what now?

MADELIN
(gesturing wildly, the tote now **balancing on the lamppost** like a
tightrope walker)
We’re doing a performance piece called **“Caffeinated Melon”**. The melon
*absorbs* the espresso, then *re‑emits* it as a vapor that… uh… *creates a
cloud of artistic fog*.

*The tote suddenly **shudders** as if something inside is trying to break free.
A faint, eerie *clunk‑clunk* reverberates, and the bag’s seam **splits** just
enough for a **tiny, glittering skull** to peek out, then retract.*

JACKALYN
(stifling a laugh)
You guys really *take* the term “art supply” seriously.

BISCUIT
(stands, sniffs the bag, then **hops back** as if the skull’s aura burned him)

JACKALYN
(to the dog)
You’re not scared of coffee, Biscuit. What’s wrong?

BISCUIT
(barks once, then sits, eyes locked on the bag)

JUSTINE
(to the dog, quietly)
Don’t. It knows.

JACKALYN
(slightly unnerved, but amused)
Alright, I’ll… *maybe* bring a spare tote for the skull.

MADELIN
(grinning, lifting the bag a little higher—now it **balances on the lamppost**
like a precarious sculpture)
We’ll be the ones with the *mel‑on*.

[Jackalyn tugs the leash. Biscuit finally stands, takes three steps forward,
then looks back once at the tote bag. A long, meaningful look.]

JACKALYN
(walking away, over her shoulder)
Text me! The cortado is supposedly life‑changing!

[She rounds the corner. She is gone. The sidewalk returns to its flat,
indifferent brightness.]

[Justine and Madelin stand in the sun for a moment, the tote still perched
against the lamppost, a faint glow pulsing from the skull’s eye‑socket.]

MADELIN
Do you think she noticed?

JUSTINE
The dog noticed.

MADELIN
The dog always notices.

JUSTINE
(after a beat)
We should not go to brunch.

MADELIN
(already walking)
We are absolutely not going to brunch.

[They walk. The tote bag **wobbles** dramatically, then *settles* with a final
satisfying *thunk* as the skull inside gives a tiny, triumphant nod. The city
continues to happen around them, completely unaware.]

FADE OUT.
