Title: The Skull on the Sidewalk
Credit: Written by The Jokesters Script Engine
Draft date: 2026-04-20

EXT. SUNLIT SIDEWALK - DAY

[Glaring midday light bleaches the concrete. Café tables spill out behind them.
 The city hums with the casual indifference of a Tuesday.]

Justine and Madelin walk into frame. Both are dressed as though they attended
a very important funeral at 3 a.m. and have not been home since.

Madelin carries a large canvas tote. It bulges in a way that is geometrically
incorrect. Something inside it is clearly skull-shaped. It clunks with each step.

Jackalyn rounds the corner. She wears a pristine ivory coat. She holds a matte
white polymer thermos. A brushed-aluminum smartwatch catches the sun. She has a
small white poodle on a silver leash. She looks like she just stepped out of a
notification for a wellness app.

JACKALYN
(beaming)
Oh my GOD. You two! I was literally just thinking about you!

JUSTINE
(flatly)
That's wild. Crazy world.

JACKALYN
(glancing at smartwatch)
I'm meeting Sophie at that new brunch place on Clement at eleven-thirty.
You should come. They do a lavender cortado.

MADELIN
(hoisting the tote higher)
Mmm.

JUSTINE
We might be busy.

JACKALYN
Busy with what? It's a Tuesday.

[Beat. The tote bag emits a low, resonant clunk as Madelin shifts her weight.]

JACKALYN
(tilting her head)
What's in the bag?

MADELIN
Art supplies.

JUSTINE
(simultaneously)
A melon.

[Silence.]

JACKALYN
...A melon.

MADELIN
A very large melon. For art.

JACKALYN
(skeptical but polite)
Okay. That's... a commitment.

[Jackalyn's poodle circles toward the tote and sniffs it once, then sits down
 and refuses to move. The poodle stares at the bag with the calm certainty of an
 animal that knows exactly what is in there.]

JACKALYN
Biscuit, come on.

[Biscuit does not come on.]

JUSTINE
(to the dog, quietly)
Don't.

JACKALYN
She's never done that before. She's usually very social.

MADELIN
She probably senses the minerals.

JACKALYN
The... minerals.

JUSTINE
In the melon.

[Long pause. A delivery truck rumbles past. The tote bag clunks again,
 heavily, of its own accord, as if something inside shifted to look around.]

JACKALYN
Are you both okay? You look like you haven't slept.

JUSTINE
We went for a drive last night.

JACKALYN
Where?

JUSTINE
The country.

JACKALYN
That sounds nice!

MADELIN
We listened to cabaret music.

JACKALYN
(uncertain)
...Fun?

JUSTINE
(nodding slowly)
We also found something.

JACKALYN
(brightening)
Like a great little roadside stand? There's one off 101 that does incredible—

JUSTINE
It was more of an excavation.

[Jackalyn looks from Justine to Madelin. Then to the bag. Then back to Justine.
 She sips her thermos. The thermos makes a very small, very normal sound.
 It is the only normal sound within a three-foot radius.]

JACKALYN
So... brunch? Eleven-thirty?

JUSTINE
(with genuine exhaustion)
Sure, Jackalyn.

MADELIN
We'll be the ones with the melon.

[Jackalyn tugs the leash. Biscuit finally stands, takes three steps forward,
 then looks back once at the tote bag. A long, meaningful look.]

JACKALYN
(walking away, over her shoulder)
Text me! The cortado is supposedly life-changing!

[She rounds the corner. She is gone. The sidewalk returns to its flat,
 indifferent brightness.]

[Justine and Madelin stand in the sun for a moment.]

MADELIN
Do you think she noticed?

JUSTINE
The dog noticed.

MADELIN
The dog always notices.

JUSTINE
(after a beat)
We should not go to brunch.

MADELIN
(already walking)
We are absolutely not going to brunch.

[They walk. The tote bag clunks. The city continues to happen around them,
 completely unaware.]

FADE OUT.
